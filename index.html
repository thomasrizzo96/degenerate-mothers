About

Degenerate Mothers play anthemic pop punk with an easycore streak: big singalong choruses, bouncy riffs, and a live show that earned them a headline slot at Boise's Knitting Factory on May 17, 2025. The Treasure Valley three-piece has spent four years building a following on the local circuit.

They're currently working with Boise producer Dale Hiscock on their upcoming EP, rolling out single by single through early 2027. The first, "I Still Remember," is out now.
